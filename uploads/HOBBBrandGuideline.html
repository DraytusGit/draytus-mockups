Heart of British Business

Brand guideline for the membership portal · v1 draft for sign off

01  Logo

The mark

The heart pairs British business (cogs) with the Union flag. Use the drop shadow version on light backgrounds. Keep clear space around it of at least the width of one cog, and never stretch, recolour or rotate it.

02  Colour

Palette

Heart Red#E1232B · primary
Flag Blue#15608F · secondary
Ink#1F2429 · text
Slate#5B646E · muted text
Silver#C9CED4 · lines, cog grey
Mist#F4F6F8 · page background

Red is the action and highlight colour, used sparingly for primary buttons and key figures. Blue carries navigation, headers and links. Everything else sits on white and the greys.
