One design, two users

The operator screen and the client portal are deliberately the same: pick the thing to work on, see what is left, and let the assistant walk you through it. The operator has several businesses to choose from; the client only ever has their own one. Neither user needs to be tech savvy, and because both screens share the same design, an improvement to one improves the other.

One thing at a time

Both screens use the same guided queue. One question, it drops off, the next appears. No hunting through menus.

Reuse what we hold

Companies House and earlier answers pre fill the questions. Some answers are suggested for the user to confirm, clearly labelled.

The queue is dynamic

A PEP answer adds the enhanced checks automatically. No extra screens to build for every branch.

Operator or client

The operator can complete anything in house, or push it to the client. Same questions, same record, either way.

Status is the point

The status view shows where things sit, not the hundred steps to get there. That is the screen people actually want.

Human still decides

Everything goes to a reviewer to sign off. The assistant is read only and never approves a client.
