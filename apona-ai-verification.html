How the score is calculated

Two scores, kept separate

Extraction confidence answers whether the engine read the document correctly. Compliance answers whether the cover is complete and current. They are reported side by side so a clear record with a lapsed schedule, or a valid schedule that was hard to read, both surface honestly.

Per-field extraction confidence

field score = image quality × source baseline × recogniser confidence × corroboration

Image quality: sharpness, glare, skew and resolution of that region.
Source baseline: printed text and barcodes start high, vaccine stickers high but sensitive to fade and placement, handwriting lower, stamps medium.
Recogniser confidence: the model's own probability for the characters returned.
Corroboration: agreement with other parts of the document lifts the score, a clash lowers it. UELN on the cover matching the ownership section, a lot number matching a known manufacturer format, a date that sits after the date of birth and on the expected annual cadence.

Rolling up to the record

The record score is a weighted aggregate, with identity and vaccination-critical fields carrying the most weight. Any single critical field below a floor pulls the record into review on its own, so a high average can never hide one bad reading of a UELN or a date.

Compliance

Run on the captured dates against a selectable rule set, which is the calculator logic for the different shows and societies. It checks the primary course, the interval between each booster, and whether the most recent booster is still in date for the chosen check date. Compliance confidence is capped by extraction confidence, so an unread date never produces false certainty.

The threshold

The auto-accept line is tunable, not fixed. Identity checking systems often run at thresholds lower than people expect, because corroboration carries the decision rather than any single reading. Setting it sensibly is what lets the bulk of every passport populate automatically while a person spends their time only on the genuinely ambiguous items.

  • Onboarding a horse becomes a confirmation step, not a data-entry step.
  • Human effort is spent only on the entries the engine is unsure about.
  • The same captured data feeds the verified record, the event checks and the real time queries with no re-keying.
Why two scores
  • Reading the document and judging the cover are different questions, so they are scored and shown separately.
  • Compliance is held to the confidence of the weakest reading it depends on.
The lever
  • The auto-accept threshold trades speed against oversight and is set by Apona, not fixed in the build.
